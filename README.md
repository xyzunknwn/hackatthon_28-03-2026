Urban Transit Sim 🚇🌿

A lightweight, turn-based public transportation simulator built entirely with vanilla HTML, JavaScript, and Tailwind CSS. Step into the role of Transit Director and build a sustainable transit network for your city.

🌟 Key Features

Build Infrastructure: Lay down Bus, Tram, Metro, and Ferry lines across a procedurally generated city grid.

Resource Management: Balance your budget against construction and ongoing maintenance costs.

Eco-Friendly Strategy: Keep your city's Carbon Footprint low while maximizing Citizen Satisfaction to win the game.

Dynamic Events: React to random turn-based events like traffic gridlocks, fuel spikes, and government grants.

Neon & Accessible UI: Enjoy a bright, high-contrast neon theme complete with a built-in color inversion toggle for accessibility.

🛠️ Tech Stack

Frontend: Vanilla HTML5 & JavaScript

Styling: Tailwind CSS (via CDN)

Icons: Lucide Icons
